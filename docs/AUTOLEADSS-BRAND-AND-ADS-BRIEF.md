# AutoLeadss brand and advertising brief

Use this document as the source of truth when creating ads, banners, landing-page copy, or Grok prompts for AutoLeadss.

## Positioning

AutoLeadss builds focused websites for small businesses in Egypt and the Gulf. The standard engagement combines a landing page, booking or lead capture, and a scripted local FAQ assistant. The commercial goal is to turn mobile visitors into qualified WhatsApp conversations.

## Approved offer

- Egypt package: 10,000 EGP.
- Outside Egypt: $200 USD.
- Payment: 50% before work starts and 50% before final handoff.
- Includes one focused landing page, up to four featured products or services, English/Arabic/bilingual support, two revision rounds, booking or lead form, local FAQ assistant, connection of an existing domain, and 14 days of post-launch fixes.
- New domain registration, large multi-page sites, more than four products, e-commerce, ongoing maintenance, dashboards, and custom integrations require a separate quote.
- Do not promise a fixed delivery time unless AutoLeadss confirms it for that project.

## Audience

Primary segments: clinics and dentists, salons and beauty studios, cafes and restaurants, gyms and fitness studios, consultants, and small service businesses in Cairo, Giza, Alexandria, Dubai, and the Gulf.

## Approved proof

- Five clearly labelled interactive demos: Qahwa House, Nile Dental, Forge, Noon Studio, and Lash Cartel.
- Live studio products: TUT, IBNI, and Virlo.
- Lash Cartel is ongoing studio work that includes UGC ads and ad management. Do not imply that those services are included in the website package.
- Never invent revenue, conversion, traffic, client counts, delivery times, testimonials, or campaign performance.

## Voice

Direct, commercially literate, calm, and specific. Short sentences. Avoid “revolutionary,” “game-changing,” “guaranteed results,” fake scarcity, and inflated AI language. Use Arabic that sounds natural in Egypt rather than formal machine translation.

## Visual system

- Background: near-black `#0A0A0B`.
- Primary accent: orange `#FF5C2A`.
- Conversion accent: WhatsApp green `#1E7E48`.
- Light editorial surfaces: `#FAFAF7` and `#EFECE4`.
- Bold sans-serif headlines, occasional editorial serif emphasis, compact mono labels.
- Use real site and demo screenshots. Do not generate fake dashboards or fabricated client results.

## Ad hooks

1. Your website should answer questions before WhatsApp does.
2. Website, booking, and FAQ assistant in one clear package.
3. Stop sending ad traffic to an Instagram profile.
4. Let customers book while you are busy.
5. Built for Arabic, English, and mobile-first local businesses.

## CTA

Primary: “Get a quote on WhatsApp.” Secondary: “Open the matching demo.”

## Recommended formats

- Meta feed: 1080 × 1350.
- Instagram square: 1080 × 1080.
- Stories/Reels: 1080 × 1920, keeping important text inside the central safe area.
- Landscape display: 1200 × 628.
