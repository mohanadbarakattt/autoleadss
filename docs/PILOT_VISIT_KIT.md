## The 20-second opening

> I built a private concept for your business because you have something customers repeat every month. It lets a customer choose a package, pay online, receive a QR membership pass, and redeem visits or balance while you see everything in one simple dashboard. This is not a generic mock-up—I shaped it around your business. Can I show you?

Do not lead with “website.” Lead with repeat revenue, prepaid demand, and an easier return visit.

## What to ask every owner

The concept prices are placeholders until these three numbers are known:

1. What does the included service or product actually cost the business?
2. How many times does a good repeat customer buy it in a normal month?
3. What unused allowance, off-peak restriction or bundle margin keeps the package profitable?

Then confirm:

- preferred merchant/payment provider and whether the account is already approved;
- who is allowed to scan/redemptions and from which devices;
- expiry, rollover, pause and refund rules;
- owner-approved logo, photographs, menu/service list and prices;
- whether launch is English, Arabic or bilingual.
