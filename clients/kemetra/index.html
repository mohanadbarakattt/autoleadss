Our story

Two sisters, one workshop.

Kemetra was created by two sisters who share a deep belief in the power of nature, intention and care. With backgrounds in natural ingredients, essential oils and traditional formulation, we focus on understanding what truly benefits the skin — and respects the environment.

“Every product is developed with simplicity and purpose, combining knowledge with hands-on craft.”

Pure ingredients, conscious care. Because your skin absorbs what you feed it.
